About AI Face Search

AI Face Search is an image-based search platform designed to help users discover visually similar images across publicly available sources on the internet.

Our goal is to make image search simple, transparent, and accessible. By allowing users to upload or capture a photo, AI Face Search analyzes visual patterns and matches them with images that are openly accessible online. We do not access private accounts, restricted platforms, or paywalled databases.

AI Face Search does not require users to create an account and does not use uploaded images for advertising, marketing, or profiling. Images are processed only to provide the search results requested by the user.

We believe in responsible use of technology. AI Face Search is built with a privacy-first mindset, focusing on minimal data retention, user control, and clear boundaries around how data is processed.

Whether you’re researching an image, verifying publicly available content, or exploring visual similarities, AI Face Search provides a straightforward and user-focused experience.

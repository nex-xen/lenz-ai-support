Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24-01-2026

Lenso.ai provides an image-based search application that allows users to upload or capture a photo to find visually similar images available on publicly accessible sources across the internet.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed and protected when you use the Lenso.ai mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

No Account Required

Lenso.ai does not require account registration. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or government-issued identifiers to use the App.

Information You Choose to Provide

To provide core functionality, Lenso.ai processes information that you voluntarily submit, including:

  • Images: Photos selected from your device gallery or captured using the camera.
  • Search Requests: Actions initiated by you to perform image similarity searches.

This information is used only to perform the search you request. Lenz.ai does not use submitted images for advertising, marketing, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

Image Search & Public Data Sources

Lenso.ai compares submitted images against publicly available images indexed from open and accessible sources on the internet.

The App does not access private accounts, paywalled services, or restricted databases. Search results may include links or references to publicly available profiles or webpages. Lenz.ai does not control, host, or modify third-party content and is not responsible for the accuracy or availability of external sources.

Image Processing & Third-Party Services

To perform image similarity matching, Lenso.ai securely transmits uploaded images to trusted third-party image processing services using encrypted connections.

Images are used only to generate search results requested by the user. They are not used for facial recognition, identity verification, advertising, or to train AI or machine-learning models. Third-party providers process images only for the requested search functionality and are not permitted to use the data for any other purpose.

Images may be retained temporarily for processing and security purposes and are automatically deleted shortly after the search is completed, typically within 60 seconds.

Data Storage & Retention

Lenso.ai follows a minimal-retention, privacy-first approach.

  • Uploaded images are processed temporarily and automatically deleted shortly after processing, typically within 60 seconds.
  • Lenso.ai does not store uploaded images on its own servers.
  • Search history, if available, is stored locally on your device.
  • Removing images, clearing app data, or uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored data.

Camera & Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to upload or capture an image. Permissions can be managed or revoked at any time through your device settings.

Technical Diagnostics

To maintain app stability and performance, Lenz.ai may collect limited technical diagnostics such as device type, operating system version, and crash or error logs. This information is anonymized, does not include uploaded images, and is not linked to your identity. Lenso.ai does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Notifications

Lenso.ai may send notifications related to app functionality or important service updates. Notifications are sent only with your explicit system-level permission and can be enabled or disabled at any time through your device settings. Lenz.ai does not send promotional or advertising notifications.

Payments & Subscriptions (If Applicable)

All purchases and subscriptions are processed securely through Apple’s App Store. Lenso.ai does not collect or store payment information and receives only anonymized subscription status confirmation from Apple. Subscription management and cancellations are handled through your Apple ID settings.

User Control

You remain in control of your data. You may remove uploaded images, clear local app data, or uninstall the App at any time. Once deleted, data cannot be recovered by Lenso.ai.

Children’s Privacy

Lenso.ai is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from children. Any such information will be promptly deleted.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. While no system can guarantee absolute security, protecting user data is a top priority.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
